Design philosophy

Built on restraint

No pure white. No pure black. No saturated accents. Just a single quiet palette, generous typography, and a system that respects your attention.

01 / Palette

Ice-blue & midnight

A cool-toned light mode and a deep navy dark mode. Never harsh, never loud.

02 / Typography

Cormorant & Inter

An academic serif for display, a clean sans for reading. Data set in JetBrains Mono.

03 / Motion

Quiet by default

Subtle transitions, minimal shadows, and corners rounded just enough to feel friendly.

04 / Focus

Content first

No gradients, no glow, no clutter. Just the material, presented clearly.
